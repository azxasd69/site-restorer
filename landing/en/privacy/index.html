Privacy policy

Updated 10 November 2026 Site lastcopy.ru 4 min read

In short

  • No accounts, forms, subscriptions or ads — we never receive your name, email or phone number.
  • The only data is aggregated visit statistics through Yandex Metrica.
  • The counter starts right away and a banner tells you so on the first visit. One button turns it off — the page reloads without statistics.
  • The refusal is remembered by your browser; statistics can be re-enabled via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

1. What is processed

This site is a static page for the Site Restorer project. There is no sign-up, no comment form, no newsletter and no ad network. We do not collect or store your contact details.

The Yandex Metrica counter loads when the page opens and processes:

DataPurposeBasis
IP address, device and browser type, screen size, languageTechnical visit statisticsNotice
Page address, referrer, time of visitKnowing which pages are read and where visitors come fromNotice
Clicks and scrolling (click map)Checking whether people reach the download buttonNotice
Cookie identifiersTelling a new visit from a returning oneNotice

The data is used only in aggregate: “how many people read this page”, “which queries brought them”. We do not build profiles and do not tie statistics to a person.

2. Cookies and retention

The site sets no cookies of its own. Cookies come only from the Yandex Metrica counter; the list and lifetimes are described in the Metrica documentation.

Your choice is stored in browser localStorage under pf-consent and never sent anywhere: notified means the notice was read, denied means statistics are off. It stays on your device until you clear site data.

Statistics retention is set in the counter’s Metrica account. We do not export raw data or keep it separately.

3. Behaviour recording (WebVisor)

The counter has WebVisor enabled: it records mouse movement, clicks and scrolling so we can see where a page is confusing. Recordings are anonymous.

The site has no input fields, so there is no text, password or payment data to record. If such fields ever appear, WebVisor masks them.

4. Who else is involved

  • Yandex — operator of Yandex Metrica, processing statistics under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. — site hosting (GitHub Pages). Like any web server it keeps technical access logs; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Data is not shared with anyone else, not sold and not used for advertising. Downloading the app and following links to GitHub, Boosty or third-party material is governed by those services’ own policies.

5. Legal basis and your rights

Statistics are collected to the extent needed to measure site traffic; the notice on your first visit tells you about it. Refusing is voluntary and does not restrict access to the site or the download.

You may:

  • turn statistics off with the “Turn off statistics” button in the notice or “Cookie settings” in the footer;
  • block cookies in your browser;
  • ask for information about processing by writing to us (see “Contacts”).

6. How to refuse

  1. On the site. Press “Turn off statistics” in the notice, or open “Cookie settings” in the footer and press the same button — the page reloads without the counter and Metrica cookies are removed. A pageview already sent before the refusal cannot be retracted.
  2. In the browser. Block third-party cookies or enable tracking protection.
  3. With an extension. Any blocker that stops mc.yandex.ru disables the counter completely.

7. Changes

If the set of data or services changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Material changes will also show the notice again.

8. Contacts

The project is open source and has no mailbox on the domain. Questions about data processing go through the repository issues, where the site history is public as well.

This policy covers the lastcopy.ru website only. The Site Restorer app runs locally, sends no telemetry and needs no internet connection except the web archive requests you trigger yourself.
